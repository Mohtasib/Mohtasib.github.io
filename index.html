01 / About

Robots that learn from what they see

I'm a machine learning scientist at Zebra Technologies, working across deep learning, reinforcement learning and robotics.

I did my PhD in Computer Science at the Lincoln Centre for Autonomous Systems (L-CAS), University of Lincoln, UK. My doctoral research was on deep reinforcement learning for robotic manipulation and grasping. It looked at how to combine human demonstrations with reinforcement learning, and how to learn reward functions from visual observations rather than engineering them for every task.

My route into robotics started with hardware. I studied mechatronics engineering at Palestine Polytechnic University and embedded systems at the University of Essex, and my first paper described a self-balancing two-wheel electric vehicle.

I'm always happy to talk about robot learning, share code from my projects, or explore a collaboration. Get in touch.

02 / Research

Three threads I keep pulling on

Learning from demonstration

Using a small number of human demonstrations to bootstrap reinforcement learning, so a robot needs far less trial and error to master a manipulation skill.

See [1] [2]

Rewards from pixels

Learning reward signals and task-success classifiers directly from camera images, instead of hand-engineering a reward function for every new task.

See [1] [3] [4]

Sequential manipulation

Scaling policy learning to multi-step tasks, where a robot has to reach several sub-goals in the right order to finish the job.

See [1]

04 / Code

Open-source projects

AWET_RL ↗

Implementation of AWET (advantage weighting and early termination), built on the Stable-Baselines3 versions of DDPG, TD3 and SAC. Code for the IROS 2022 paper [2].

PythonReinforcement learning RewardLearning ↗

Eight neural classifiers, from fully convolutional nets to Transformers and domain-adaptation models, for learning the rewards of five robotic manipulation tasks. Datasets included.

PythonReward learning Custom-Gym ↗

Custom Gym environments: reacher and pusher tasks, each with dense-reward and sparse-reward variants.

PythonEnvironments Static_Features_Classifier ↗

An attention-based RNN classifier for the static features of point-cloud clusters.
